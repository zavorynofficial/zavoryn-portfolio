Got Questions?

How fast is Same-Day delivery?
If you book before 12:00 PM local time, your package will be delivered by 6:00 PM the exact same day within our major metro coverage areas.
Do you offer integrations for Shopify/WooCommerce?
Yes, our Business Logistics accounts include access to our REST API, with direct plugins available for Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento for automatic label generation.
How accurate is the live tracking?
Our delivery fleet is equipped with GPS trackers that update every 60 seconds, ensuring you and your customers know exactly where the package is.
What is the maximum weight for a standard parcel?
Standard express parcels can weigh up to 30kg. Anything over this weight or requiring palletization falls under our Bulk Shipments category.
Are my shipments insured?
Yes, all standard shipments are automatically insured up to $100. Additional declared value coverage can be purchased during the booking process.
How do I open a Business Account?
Simply click "Talk to Logistics Team" or reach out via WhatsApp. A dedicated account manager will set up your discounted rates based on your monthly volume within 24 hours.
