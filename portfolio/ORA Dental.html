Everything you need to know before your first visit.

In-office whitening typically takes 60–90 minutes for immediate results. Take-home kits may take 1–2 weeks of daily use. Results vary based on your starting shade and lifestyle habits.
The procedure is performed under local anesthesia so you won't feel pain during treatment. Some mild discomfort is normal afterward, but most patients find it manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.
