Client Questions

What product lines do you use?
We partner with exclusively premium, cruelty-free luxury brands. For hair, we utilize Oribe and Kérastase. For skincare, we use Biologique Recherche and SkinCeuticals.
How far in advance should I book for bridal services?
We recommend booking bridal consultations 6 to 9 months in advance to ensure availability for your wedding day and adequate time for any preparatory skin or hair color regimens.
What is your cancellation policy?
We require 48 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made within 48 hours will incur a fee equal to 50% of the reserved service amount.
